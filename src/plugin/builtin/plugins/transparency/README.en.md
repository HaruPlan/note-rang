Make the note window subtly see-through. The screen behind it shows through just a bit, like a glass note pad on your desk.

## How to use

- Hover over a note to reveal the toolbar. Drag the slider to set transparency (30%–100%).
- This setting is saved per note — keep some solid and others faint, however you like.

> 💡 Turning this plugin off removes the slider and the note goes back to fully solid. Your transparency setting is remembered, and comes back as soon as you turn it on again.

🍎 macOS only. On other platforms the slider doesn't appear and notes always stay fully solid.
