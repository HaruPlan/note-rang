Links your notes together. Type `[[Title]]` in the body and it becomes a link that jumps straight to the note with that title.

## How to use

- Wrap the title of the note you want to link to in double brackets: `[[Meeting Notes]]` → it turns into an underlined link.
- Click the link to open the note window with that title.
- Typing just `[[` brings up autocomplete with the titles of your current notes. Pick one and `[[Title]]` fills in for you.

> 💡 While your cursor is on that line, the raw `[[Meeting Notes]]` text stays visible. Move to another line and it turns into the link — that's not a bug, it just means you're currently editing that line.
