Hides content that shouldn't be visible right away, like a password or an answer. Hover to reveal it.

## How to use

- Wrap the text you want to hide with double `||`: `||the answer is 42||` → it's blurred out, and hovering reveals it.

> 💡 Once it's done, the content stays hidden until you hover over it. If it's not hiding, your cursor is probably still on that line — move to another line and it'll hide again.
