Counts the words and characters in the note you're working on, live.

## How to use

- Two status items, "N words" and "M chars", sit on the note toolbar — no need
  to press anything; they refresh themselves when the note opens or saves
  (autosave included).
- Click either one to copy that exact text (e.g. "12 words") to the clipboard.
- You can move where they sit in **Settings › Appearance › Toolbar Layout**,
  including moving them separately or hiding just one.
